## Their public record

Linq Photonics states that it is developing photonic control systems for quantum computing. Its founding team consists of Rasmus Bankwitz, Jade Robinson, and Julius Römer; the team page lists Pernice as founding advisor and characterises him as a serial founder.

Source: [Linq team page](https://www.linq-photonics.com/team).

## What this means for our project

Linq is not a direct TDRC comparator. It matters because it illustrates a company emerging from the same broader research ecosystem but aimed at a tightly bounded systems problem: photonic control hardware for quantum-computing infrastructure.

## Our risks and checks

- If we call Linq a Pernice-founded company, then we would misstate the company’s own role description. The documented connection is founding advisor.
- If we treat quantum-photonics control as an immediate SBS-reservoir market, then we would jump over task, interface, and integration validation that has not been done.
