## Their public record

Pixel Photonics identifies Wolfram Pernice as co-founder, scientific advisor, and advisory-board member. It describes its detector technology as integrated photonic structures, waveguides, and SNSPDs developed for quantum computing, quantum-secure communication, microscopy, metrology, and sensing.

Sources: [Pixel team page](https://www.pixelphotonics.com/en/company/our-team/), [Pixel / SPRIND project profile](https://www.sprind.org/en/actions/projects/pixel-photonics), [founding announcement](https://www.pixelphotonics.com/download-file?file_code=7dc014224b&file_id=411).

## Independent technical evidence

Pernice's academic work includes integrated nanophotonic and detector-related research, but this note does not yet identify a single peer-reviewed paper that independently validates Pixel's complete current SNSPD product platform. The company and public-funding sources substantiate its spin-out origin and stated direction; they do not by themselves establish current commercial performance.

## What this means for our project

Pixel is not a direct computational competitor. It is nevertheless a useful regional commercialisation case: an academic photonics technology has been given a sharply bounded product—integrated single-photon detection—rather than being offered as generic "quantum photonics." This is the kind of application specificity our SBS spin-off must achieve.

## Our risks and checks

- If we treat a shared founder or university ecosystem as a route to collaboration, then we would overstate the relationship. No collaboration interest or access has been established.
- If our product narrative remains broad while Pixel's is detector-specific, then we will lack the product boundary that makes a deep-tech spin-off intelligible to partners and funders.
