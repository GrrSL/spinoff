## Purpose

This folder is an internal landscape of organisations relevant to the SBS-enhanced TDRC spin-off. A **peer** can be either:

- a comparator whose product, technical strategy, commercialisation route, or systems integration offers a useful benchmark; or
- a prospective partner able to provide a native analogue signal source, realistic data, application requirements, integration capability, or a route to field validation.

The notes are not a claim that any organisation is a direct competitor, prospective customer, or willing collaborator. They are evidence-backed starting points for outreach and positioning.

## Reading the comparison correctly

The proposed spin-off is not presently a like-for-like replacement for data-centre AI accelerators. Its differentiated hypothesis is **dynamic processing close to a native analogue optical or RF signal, with a compact output**, using time-delay reservoir dynamics and SBS-controlled functionality. Most computing peers below instead target matrix multiplication, general AI/HPC acceleration, or optical interconnects.

This distinction is useful rather than defensive: it identifies both the gap the project must validate and the product-integration standards it should learn from. See [[Project_Memory]] for the DAC-before-processing and compact-output framing, and [[Technical_Specification]] for the current experimental constraints.

## Research method and update rule

- Facts are drawn primarily from the organisation's own current pages or primary technical publications, with a retrieval date in each note.
- Vendor performance statements remain attributed to the vendor and are not independently validated here.
- The sections titled **What this means for our project**, **Our positioning relative to them**, **What collaboration could test for us**, and **Our outreach hypothesis** are internal assessments, not claims about the organisation's intent.
- Before external use, re-check company status, product availability, claims, named contacts, and any proposed relationship.
