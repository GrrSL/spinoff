## Snapshot

- **Organisation:** EDWATEC (Switzerland)
- **Role in this landscape:** active-photonics / integrated-gain peer and potential future component-path reference.
- **Named research connection:** Tobias J. Kippenberg is publicly listed as co-founder, scientific advisor, and board member.
- **Evidence checked:** 2026-10-10.

## Their public record

EDWATEC states that it commercialises erbium-doped photonic integrated circuits for on-chip optical amplification. It identifies itself as a 2023 EPFL spin-off and says its platform targets transceivers, data and telecommunications, fibre sensing, and satellite communications.

Source: [EDWATEC company and team page](https://www.edwatec.com/about-us).

## What this means for our project

Integrated gain is a directly relevant future subsystem question because the present SBS setup requires substantial pump power. EDWATEC does not solve that challenge for us, but it is a useful reference for how active optical functionality can be productised in a PIC-based ecosystem.

## Our risks and checks

- If integrated optical gain is assumed to solve the current pump-power challenge, then we would conflate signal gain with the SBS pump-power and spectral-control requirements. The required optical budget must be demonstrated for the actual architecture.
