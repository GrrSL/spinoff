## Their public record

coreX describes coreDAQ as a driverless optical-power-monitoring and acquisition system designed for photonic-circuit and co-packaged-optics characterisation. Its listed founders are Ravi Pradip and Moritz Linnebacher; Pernice is named as scientific mentor.

Source: [coreX company page](https://core-instrumentation.com/).

## What this means for our project

This is not a computing competitor. It is an instructive example of a commercially focused subsystem for a bottleneck in photonic development and production. If the SBS platform moves towards PIC integration, comparable measurement and calibration infrastructure will become part of the engineering path.

## Our risks and checks

- If our project assumes that laboratory instrumentation transfers directly to a deployable product, then calibration, monitoring, and production-test requirements may emerge too late.
- If we represent Pernice as coreX founder, then we would misstate the published team roles.
