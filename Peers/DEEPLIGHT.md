## Their public record

Kippenberg's CV identifies DEEPLIGHT as a 2021 co-founded company commercialising integrated photonic light sources based on piezoelectric actuators and ultra-low-loss silicon-nitride PICs.

Source: [Kippenberg CV](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/k-lab/wp-content/uploads/2024_09_CV_Kippenberg_with-funding.pdf).

## What this means for our project

An integrated optical source is a potentially valuable component for a future compact SBS-reservoir product, particularly where frequency control and optical-system packaging dominate the engineering burden. DEEPLIGHT is therefore a technology and supply-chain reference rather than a reservoir-computing peer.

## Our risks and checks

- If a compact source is assumed compatible with the SBS frequency-control requirements by default, then linewidth, tuning range, relative-frequency stability, and optical power may later become blocking constraints. See [[Technical_Specification]].
