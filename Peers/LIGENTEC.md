## Snapshot

- **Organisation:** LIGENTEC (Switzerland)
- **Role in this landscape:** integrated-photonics foundry and potential future implementation enabler.
- **Named research connection:** Tobias J. Kippenberg is publicly documented as a co-founder.
- **Evidence checked:** 2026-10-10.

## Their public record

Kippenberg's CV identifies LIGENTEC as a 2016 co-founded company commercialising tightly confining ultra-low-loss silicon-nitride photonic integrated circuits. This is a platform / manufacturing business, not an optical-computing product.

Source: [Kippenberg CV](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/k-lab/wp-content/uploads/2024_09_CV_Kippenberg_with-funding.pdf).

## What this means for our project

LIGENTEC is relevant to a long-term integrated platform route because low-loss SiN circuits are compatible with delay, resonator, and nonlinear-photonics building blocks. Its relevance is enabling infrastructure, not proof that an SBS reservoir can be transferred to SiN without a separate design and experimental programme.

## Our risks and checks

- If we assume a foundry platform removes all system constraints, then fibre coupling, pump handling, nonlinear interaction strength, and control requirements may be underestimated.
