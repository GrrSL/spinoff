## Snapshot

- **Organisation:** LUXTELLIGENCE (Switzerland)
- **Role in this landscape:** lithium-niobate PIC-foundry peer and possible future enabling platform.
- **Named research connection:** Tobias J. Kippenberg is publicly documented as a co-founder.
- **Evidence checked:** 2026-10-10.

## Their public record

Kippenberg's CV identifies LUXTELLIGENCE as a 2022 co-founded lithium-niobate pure-play foundry based on deep-etching technology developed from his laboratory's work. The stated purpose is broader access to ferroelectric PIC technologies.

Source: [Kippenberg CV](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/k-lab/wp-content/uploads/2024_09_CV_Kippenberg_with-funding.pdf).

## What this means for our project

Lithium niobate is relevant as a potential future route for modulation and nonlinear optical functionality. LUXTELLIGENCE is worth tracking as a manufacturing / ecosystem reference; it is not evidence that our SBS-enabled TDRC should or can immediately migrate to that platform.

## Our risks and checks

- If material-platform optionality is presented as an established development path, then the proposal could overstate its integration readiness. Platform selection requires an explicit comparison of nonlinearity, loss, pump requirements, delay architecture, and packaging.
