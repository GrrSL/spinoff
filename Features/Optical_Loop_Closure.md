## Feature in brief

The long-term ambition is to remove the electronic part of the feedback loop and maintain the relevant feedback process optically.

## Potential value

- Reduce repeated optical-electrical and electrical-optical conversions.
- Strengthen the low-energy proposition of optical neuromorphic processing.

## Status

- This is a longer-term development direction and is not a committed objective of the current 18-month grant.

## Bottlenecks and risks

- If SBS alone, pump depletion, or another optical mechanism cannot provide the required nonlinear response at usable operating conditions, then closing the loop optically does not create a functional reservoir.
- If input injection, readout, control, or multichannel coupling cannot be implemented without restoring the removed electronic loop, then the feature does not deliver its intended reduction in repeated conversion.
- If laser-to-loop locking, particularly with [[Features/Pump_Recuperation]], is too unstable or complex, then the optical loop cannot remain at a usable operating point.
- If loop loss, noise, drift, or unwanted modes prevent sustained stable dynamics, then the optical feedback loop will not operate as a reliable computational system.
- If the feature is treated as a required 18-month deliverable rather than a longer-term option, then it creates disproportionate execution risk for the grant.
