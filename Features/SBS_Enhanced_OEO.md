## Feature in brief

The SBS-enhanced OEO uses a nonlinear fibre and counter-propagating pump to introduce a narrowband, pump-controlled SBS interaction into an optoelectronic feedback loop.

## Potential value

- Combine filtering and amplification functions in one SBS-enabled element.
- Control the filter position through pump configuration.
- Provide nonlinear dynamics, including experimentally observed bifurcation and pulse-triggered chaotic behaviour.
- Form the common platform for pump-programmed masking and multichannel operation.

## Bottlenecks and risks

- If a target application's relevant signal bandwidth or temporal dynamics exceed the approximately 45 MHz / 30 ns SBS response recorded in [[Technical_Specification]], then the present SBS-enhanced OEO cannot process that application directly.
- If temperature change, frequency drift, pump fluctuations, or fibre variation move the SBS response away from the intended operating point, then gain, filtering, and nonlinear dynamics become unstable.
- If gain, noise, dynamic range, or power consumption prevent a usable signal-to-noise ratio at the required operating point, then the platform cannot deliver reliable reservoir output.
- If the platform demonstrates oscillator behaviour but not a task-relevant reservoir-computing advantage, then SBS enhancement has not been shown to support the proposed technology.
- If flexibility for application experiments requires a setup too large or complex to integrate, then the platform cannot progress from laboratory demonstrator towards the [[Project_Memory#P-001 — Prototype trajectory]].
