## Application in brief

Free-space optical links transmit information through air or space. Turbulence, pointing variation, atmospheric effects, and link dynamics can impair the received signal.

## Why it fits the project

- The signal is optical by nature.
- A reservoir could potentially classify channel states, detect impairment, predict near-term link behaviour, or perform task-specific pre-processing.
- The project already has relevant nonlinear-channel and time-series-processing themes.

## Bottlenecks and risks

- If no specific link impairment and pre-ADC task are selected, then free-space optical communication remains a generic market label rather than a testable application.
- If channel dynamics or the optical waveform exceed the SBS temporal response recorded in [[Technical_Specification]], then the current platform cannot process the relevant information directly.
- If the useful information exists only after standard coherent or direct detection, then the system does not receive the native optical input needed to justify optical pre-processing.
- If pointing variation, turbulence, weather, background light, or link intermittency dominate the signal more strongly than the proposed reservoir can tolerate, then task performance will not be operationally reliable.
- If access to a suitable testbed or partner is unavailable within the project period, then this direction cannot progress beyond simulation or laboratory emulation.
