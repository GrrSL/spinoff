## Application in brief

Optical communication systems must compensate nonlinear distortion and dispersion introduced during fibre transmission. Nonlinear Channel Equalisation (NCE) is the project's demonstrated baseline task.

## Why it fits the project

- NCE has already been used to test the system.
- The signal originates in an optical channel.
- The project could investigate reservoir-based equalisation or related optical-link monitoring functions.

## Bottlenecks and risks

- If the relevant communication task requires processing bandwidth beyond the SBS response in [[Technical_Specification]], then the present SBS platform cannot be used directly for that link.
- If the architecture requires DAC solely to recreate a suitable analogue input from already digitised traffic, then it violates the project-level energy rationale in [[Project_Memory#C-009 — Critical assessment of analogue radio-over-fibre]].
- If no advantage in power, latency, throughput, adaptability, deployment location, or form factor can be shown against established electronic DSP, then reservoir-based equalisation has no credible adoption case.
- If the test channel and error metric do not represent a partner-relevant link, then a benchmark result cannot support a commercial or translational claim.
